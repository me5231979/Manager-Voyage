Manager Voyage at a glance.

One structured path for every Vanderbilt manager from the day they start: the compliance courses the law requires, a Foundations course on what Vanderbilt expects of a manager, an optional four-week cohort to practice with a real team, and a portal that stays with them for the rest of their career. Built on one research-based framework and measured with a survey before the program and 90 days after.

The path

The first 60 days, then the choice to go further.

Day 1Courses assigned in Oracle Learning from the HR record. Assessment taken. Portal and My voyage open.
Day 7The 35-minute Foundation course, You Are a Manager Now, complete.
Day 30Law-required compliance courses done. Systems and Vanderbilt & Nashville modules done.
Day 60All compliance and Foundations complete and recorded against the job profile. Cohort invitation sent.
After 60Optional four-week cohort, offered quarterly. Portal used throughout.
90 daysAssessment taken again. The change in a manager’s own answers is the measure.

The four components

01 Required

Compliance

Days 1 to 60 · renewed annually

The legal and policy courses every manager must complete, matched to the state where the team works. Assigned automatically on Day 1; no enrollment step.

Law-required courses due in 30 days, policy courses in 60. Oracle Learning holds the record.

02 Strongly recommended

Manager Foundations

Days 1 to 60 · course by Day 7

Three parts, in order: the assessment, the 35-minute Foundation course, and short micro modules in Oracle Learning.

Four tracks: Systems and Vanderbilt & Nashville by Day 30, People and Processes by Day 60.

03 Optional, recommended

Four-week cohort

After Day 60 · quarterly · 100 seats

Guided practice with the manager’s own team. Each week: a core module, a learning lab, a scenario studio, and a Friday discussion.

Managers build a Manager Playbook and present it at the capstone.

04 Always available

Manager Portal

From Day 1, for a career

One site with every template, policy link, and contact from the course and the cohort, plus who to call for same-day routes.

Maintained by People, Culture and Belonging.

The framework

Four categories, fifteen behaviors.

Task-orientedGet the work done
  • Clarifying
  • Planning
  • Monitoring operations
  • Problem solving
Relations-orientedTake care of your people
  • Supporting
  • Developing
  • Recognizing
  • Empowering
Change-orientedMake things better
  • Advocating change
  • Envisioning change
  • Encouraging innovation
  • Collective learning
ExternalConnect your team
  • Networking
  • External monitoring
  • Representing

Measuring impact

Reach and change.

ReachShare of managers enrolled, from Oracle Learning. Target: 80% of M1 to M5 managers.
ChangeA ten-question survey on how often managers do the behaviors, taken before the program and 90 days after. The difference is the measure.
ThenAn optional six-month follow-up, and in a later phase the same questions answered by direct reports.
