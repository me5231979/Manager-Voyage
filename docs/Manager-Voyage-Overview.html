Manager Voyage.

Vanderbilt University’s development program for managers. It gives every manager the same structured path from the day they start: the compliance courses the law requires, a Foundations course on what Vanderbilt expects of a manager, an optional four-week cohort to practice with a real team, and a portal of resources that stays with them throughout their career.

The four components

01 Required

Compliance

The legal and policy courses every manager must complete, assigned automatically in Oracle Learning and matched to where the team works.

02 Strongly recommended

Manager Foundations

What Vanderbilt expects of a manager: a short self-assessment, a 35-minute course, and micro modules in four tracks.

03 Optional, recommended

Four-week cohort

Guided practice with the manager’s own team, offered quarterly. Managers build a Manager Playbook and present it at the capstone.

04 Always available

Manager Portal

One site with every template, policy link, and contact from the course and the cohort, including who to call.

The framework

Four categories, fifteen behaviors.

Manager Voyage draws on a research-based framework of effective leadership behavior that organizes what strong managers do into four broad categories and fifteen specific behaviors. The Foundations course introduces these behaviors explicitly and gives managers opportunities to practice them. The cohort gives structured practice. The portal provides the tools. The survey measures how often managers report doing them.

Measuring impact

Reach and change.

Reach is the share of managers enrolled, recorded in Oracle Learning. Change is a ten-question survey on how often managers do these behaviors, taken before the program and again 90 days after. The difference in a manager’s own answers is the measure.
