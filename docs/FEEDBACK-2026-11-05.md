## Context the group was given

- Internal fill rates are well below benchmark because the manager bench is thin; the program exists to build it.
- The Vanderbilt Manager Standard is adapted from Yukl: get the work done through the team, take care of your people, make things better, connect your team to the rest of Vanderbilt.
- The first 30 days: compliance courses required, the Foundations course strongly encouraged, micro courses for processes that change (FMLA, PTO, timecards, hiring, expenses).
- After 60 days in good standing: the four-week cohort, 25 managers a quarter through PCB, with bespoke cohorts for large units such as DAR.
