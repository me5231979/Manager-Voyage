Program overview

Sixty days that used to be left to chance.

Every time Vanderbilt promotes or hires a manager, a clock starts. The first 60 days set the patterns that define their leadership for years, and until now there was no standard for them. Manager Voyage gives every new manager the same sequenced path, assigned automatically in Oracle Learning the day the role begins, tracked on one dashboard, and backed by a portal and a set of people who never go away.

One framework behind every component

Fifteen behaviors, four categories, one standard.

Everything in Manager Voyage is built on Gary Yukl’s taxonomy of effective leadership behavior. His research sorted what effective managers actually do into four categories and fifteen behaviors. Foundations teaches them by name. The cohort practices them with a real team. The portal equips them. The pulse measures six behaviors drawn from them, so the program and the measure speak the same language.

Task-oriented

Get the work done, through your team.

  • Clarifying (say it)
  • Planning (plan it)
  • Monitoring operations (check it)
  • Problem solving (fix it)
Relations-oriented

Take care of your people.

  • Supporting (listen and help)
  • Developing (grow them)
  • Recognizing (thank them)
  • Empowering (trust them)
Change-oriented

Make things better.

  • Advocating change (explain the why)
  • Envisioning change (describe where you are going)
  • Encouraging innovation (let people try)
  • Facilitating collective learning (look back)
External

Connect your team to the rest of Vanderbilt.

  • Networking (know the people)
  • External monitoring (watch for what is coming)
  • Representing (speak up for your team)

How each component ties to the framework.

01 Compliance

The legal floor under address issues: what a manager must recognize, route, and report. Harassment prevention, Title IX, the Clery Act, accommodations, and privacy give monitoring and problem solving the force of law behind them.

02 Foundations

Teaches the taxonomy itself. Four categories, fifteen behaviors, each in Yukl’s words and in plain words, with what to stop and what to do instead. The Manager Effectiveness Assessment, taken before and 90 days after, rates the six behaviors built from them.

03 Cohort

Practices the categories with a real team, one focus a week: relations and psychological safety in week 1, clarifying and hard conversations in week 2, developing and empowering in week 3, monitoring operations and performance in week 4. The Playbook is the written record.

04 Portal

The templates, policy links, and contacts for all four categories, always on. When a manager needs to represent their team, route a concern, or plan a change, the tools are already there.

The six-item pulse is described as informed by Yukl’s leadership-behavior framework. It is an adaptation for Vanderbilt, not a validated Yukl instrument, and the program does not claim otherwise.

How it was built

On the bedrock of a wide pilot and a subject-matter expert group.

Manager Voyage was not written in a room and shipped. It was shaped by the people who handle the calls new managers make, tested with managers across the university, and rebuilt from what they said.

The expert group

Seven divisions shaping the content.

Human Capital Management, Employee Relations, Equal Opportunity and Access, Leave, Compliance, Title IX, and the Futures Learning Hub set the routing rules, the who-to-call map, and the standards for what a manager must know by Day 30 and Day 60. Every same-day route in the program is theirs.

The pilot · launched March 26, 2026

Fifteen managers, eight business units.

A pilot cohort ran the full journey: the assessment, the Foundation course, the micro modules, and the four-week program with its labs, rehearsals, Playbook, and capstone. Their feedback drove the current design, from the single-page dashboard to the one-Core-module-a-week cohort rhythm.

Measures of impact

Short, behavioral, and honest.

Seat time proves nothing. Manager Voyage measures two things: reach, from enrollment, and change, from the same six-item pulse asked immediately before the program and 90 days after completion. Managers rate each statement from never to consistently.

1 · Pre

Immediately before Manager Voyage.

Six statements, a 1 to 5 scale from never to consistently. This is the Manager Effectiveness Assessment the Foundation course asks the manager to review.

2 · Post

Ninety days after completion.

The exact same six items. The difference, per manager and across the cohort, is the measure of change.

3 · Sustain

Optional six-month pulse.

Tests whether the behavior change sticks once the program is out of sight.

What success looks like.

  • 80% of M1 to M5 managers enrolled in Manager Voyage.
  • The percentage of managers improving on at least four of the six behaviors.
  • The average pre to post change across the six behaviors.
  • The behavior with the greatest gain, and the behavior that needs more support.
Next evolution

Ask the team the same six.

Manager self-report shows perceived change. Direct reports answering the same six questions show whether people are actually experiencing better management.

Where the return shows up

Engagement, cases, and the bench.

Managers are the single greatest influence on team engagement. The most avoidable Employee Relations and Equal Opportunity and Access cases start with a manager never taught to hold a hard conversation or to escalate. And one standard across 39 business units means promotion and succession decisions rest on the same evidence.

Measurement guardrail: describe the pulse as informed by Yukl’s leadership-behavior framework. Do not call this six-item adaptation a validated Yukl instrument unless a validated instrument is used and its requirements are met.

RequiredDays 1 to 60 · Annual cadence
What is assigned, by work location Rendered from the program data

The state a manager’s team works in sets the list. Courses marked required by law carry a deadline; the rest are Vanderbilt policy. Supervisors take the supervisor version in addition to the all-staff version.

    Every location, every manager

      Logistics
      • Assignment. Automatic in Oracle Learning the day the HR record shows a management role. No enrollment step.
      • Delivery. OpenSesame courses inside Oracle Learning, each ending with a knowledge check Oracle records.
      • Deadlines. Most legal courses are due within 30 days of role entry, advisory courses within 60. My voyage shows the dates and flags anything at risk.
      • Renewal. Annual for most courses, biennial where the state allows. Oracle reassigns on the cadence.
      • Record. Oracle Learning holds the official completion. The dashboard’s checks are the manager’s own and sync when it runs inside Oracle.
