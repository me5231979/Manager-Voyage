Manager Voyage.

Manager Voyage is Vanderbilt University’s development program for managers. It gives every new manager the same structured path from the day they start: the compliance courses the law requires, a Foundations course on what Vanderbilt expects of a manager, an optional four-week cohort to practice those skills with a real team, and a portal of resources that stays available throughout their career. The program is built on one research-based framework and is measured with a survey taken before the program and again 90 days after.

Program overview

What Manager Voyage is.

A manager’s first 60 days shape how they will lead for years, and until now Vanderbilt had no common standard for that period. Manager Voyage provides one. Courses are assigned automatically in Oracle Learning on the manager’s first day, progress is tracked on a single dashboard called My voyage, and the people a new manager needs to reach are listed in one place.

The framework

The framework behind the program.

The Manager Voyage program is built on Gary Yukl’s taxonomy of effective leadership behavior, a research-based model that sorts what effective managers do into four categories and fifteen specific behaviors. The Foundations course teaches these behaviors by name. The cohort gives managers structured practice in them. The portal provides the tools to carry them out. The survey measures how often managers report doing them.

Task-oriented

Getting the work done through the team.

  • Clarifying (say it)
  • Planning (plan it)
  • Monitoring operations (check it)
  • Problem solving (fix it)
Relations-oriented

Taking care of the people on the team.

  • Supporting (listen and help)
  • Developing (grow them)
  • Recognizing (thank them)
  • Empowering (trust them)
Change-oriented

Improving how the team works.

  • Advocating change (explain the why)
  • Envisioning change (describe where you are going)
  • Encouraging innovation (let people try)
  • Facilitating collective learning (look back)
External

Connecting the team to the rest of Vanderbilt.

  • Networking (know the people)
  • External monitoring (watch for what is coming)
  • Representing (speak up for your team)

How the program was built

Developed with a pilot and an expert group.

Manager Voyage was developed with the offices that new managers rely on, and tested with managers across the university before its wide launch.

The expert group

Subject-matter experts from seven divisions.

They defined the routing rules, the who-to-call directory, and what a manager must be able to do by Day 30 and by Day 60.

The pilot · March 26, 2026

Fifteen managers from eight business units.

They completed the full program. Their feedback shaped the current design.

Measuring impact

How we measure impact.

Manager Voyage measures two things: reach and change. Reach is the share of managers enrolled, recorded in Oracle Learning. Change is measured with a ten-question survey covering six management behaviors, taken before the program and again 90 days after completion. The difference in a manager’s own answers shows whether their day-to-day behavior has changed.

Before the program

Ten questions, six behaviors, rated 1 to 5 from never to consistently.

90 days after completion

The same ten questions. The change in answers is the measure.

Six months, optional

A third survey shows whether the change has lasted.

The six behaviors the survey measures
BehaviorWhat we ask
Create clarityI make priorities, expectations, and what good looks like clear.Clarifying, planning
Coach and developI give timely feedback and help people build capability through their work.Monitoring operations, developing
Build trustI create an environment where people can speak up, ask questions, and learn from mistakes.Supporting, recognizing, networking
Empower othersI give people appropriate ownership and authority to make decisions.Empowering, encouraging innovation
Address issuesI address performance, people, or process problems early and constructively.Problem solving, monitoring operations, representing
Lead changeI connect change to the bigger picture and help my team adapt, learn, and try new ways of working.Advocating change, collective learning, external monitoring

The targets.

0180% enrolled

Eighty percent of M1 to M5 managers enrolled in the program.

02Four of six improved

Each manager improving on at least four of the six behaviors between the two surveys.

03Most and least

The behavior that improved most, and the one that needs more support, identified for each cohort.

04The team’s view

In a later phase, direct reports answer the same questions about their manager.

The survey is informed by Yukl’s leadership-behavior framework. It is an adaptation for Vanderbilt and is not a validated Yukl instrument.

Component 01

Compliance courses.

These are the legal and policy courses every manager must complete. Oracle Learning assigns them on the manager’s first day, based on the state where their team works.

RequiredDays 1 to 60 · Renewed annually
What is assigned, by location

Courses required by law carry a deadline. The others are Vanderbilt policy. Supervisors complete both the all-staff version and the supervisor version.

    Assigned in every location

      Logistics
      • Assignment. Courses are assigned automatically in Oracle Learning when the HR record shows a management role. There is no enrollment step.
      • Delivery. Courses are delivered through OpenSesame inside Oracle Learning, and each ends with a knowledge check.
      • Deadlines. Courses required by law are due within 30 days; policy courses within 60. My voyage shows the dates and flags anything at risk.
      • Renewal. Most courses renew every year, some every two years where the state allows.
      • Record. Oracle Learning holds the official record of completion.

      Component 03

      The four-week cohort.

      The cohort is where managers practice the skills from Foundations with their own teams. It runs for four weeks, follows the same weekly rhythm, and serves one hundred managers each quarter.

      Optional but recommendedQuarterly · 100 seats
      Who can join, and when

      The cohort is open to any manager who has completed the compliance courses and Manager Foundations. Oracle sends the invitation when the Day 60 requirements are complete.

      Units outside the central budget are billed per seat.

      The Manager Playbook connects the four weeks. Managers draft one section each week and present the finished Playbook at the capstone.
