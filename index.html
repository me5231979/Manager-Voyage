What is Manager Voyage?

Every day Vanderbilt promotes or hires a new manager, a clock starts. The first 60 days set the patterns that define their leadership for years. Manager Voyage is not a training program. It is a management standard: the first structured, sequenced, institution-wide development journey for every manager at Vanderbilt.

The voyage

  • Day 1. Oracle assigns the required components the day the role begins. The Manager Portal opens.
  • Days 1 to 60. Compliance courses and the Manager Responsibilities Course, on the manager's own schedule.
  • Day 60. Completion recorded in Oracle. The cohort invitation follows.
  • The cohort. Four weeks, in person and blended, from compliant to capable.

The unmanaged window

Sixty days left to chance.

Without structure, the first 60 days go the same way every time: compliance gaps, avoidable Employee Relations and Equal Opportunity and Access cases, and managers who were never shown what the role actually requires.

What it is

Four components. One journey.

Two are required and assigned automatically. Two are always available. Oracle Learning does the assigning and the tracking, so nothing depends on a manual handoff.

01 · Required · Days 1 to 60

Compliance Courses

Twenty state-specific courses, legal and advisory, matched to the manager's work location and delivered through OpenSesame inside Oracle. Compliance is the floor, not the bar.

02 · Required · Days 1 to 60

Manager Responsibilities Course

The operating manual for the role. A 15-minute foundation course, then 22 micro modules of 15 minutes or less across four tracks: Systems, People, Processes, and Vanderbilt and Nashville. Simulators and scenario studios, not slide decks. About four hours in total.

03 · Always on

Manager Portal

Every template, framework, policy link, and contact in one web hub. Open from Day 1, before any course is complete, and never taken away. It grows with each cohort cycle.

04 · Optional · Quarterly

Four-Week Cohort

One hundred managers a quarter, open to anyone who has finished both required components. An in-person kickoff, four blended weeks with AI rehearsal and peer labs, and an in-person capstone.

How the journey flows

The system does the work. The manager does the learning.

Compliance tells a manager what the law requires. The Manager Responsibilities Course tells them what Vanderbilt requires. The cohort turns a compliant manager into a capable one.

Day 1 in role

Assigned on arrival

The HR record shows a management role. Oracle assigns the compliance courses and the Manager Responsibilities Course. Manager Portal access begins immediately.

Days 1 to 30

Systems and story

Foundation course, Systems track, and Vanderbilt and Nashville track. By Day 30 the manager can approve, hire, and explain the mission.

Days 31 to 60

People and process

People track and Processes track, every simulator passed. By Day 60 the manager can run a 1:1, handle a leave request, and document a concern.

Day 60

Recorded and invited

Completion is recorded in Oracle against the manager's job profile and triggers the next quarterly cohort invitation. Voluntary. Units outside the central budget are billed per seat.

Cohort and beyond

From compliant to capable

Four weeks of live sessions, AI practice, and peer labs, with deliverables recorded in Oracle. The Portal stays with the manager for good.

The four-week cohort

Where skill development happens.

Each week pairs a core module with AI conversation rehearsal in Yoodli, a facilitated peer lab in SparkWise, and a section of the manager's own Playbook. FLH-built AI agents, grounded in Vanderbilt policy, are available any hour for guidance and conversation prep.

Week 1

What it means to manage

In-person kickoff at the Student Life Center. Management identity and psychological safety. Establishes the why before the how.

Week 2

Goal setting, 1:1s, difficult conversations

Rehearsed 1:1s and hard conversations. Builds daily operating discipline.

Week 3

Coaching and mentoring

Delegation and coach-or-correct peer labs. Shifts the manager from directing to developing.

Week 4

Performance and developing talent

Performance and talent conversations, then the in-person capstone. Closes the loop from learning to leading.

What every participant produces

Manager Playbook. A written personal operating system: philosophy, 1:1 framework, feedback approach, delegation plan, performance practice.

Integrated Management Model. A one-page model of how the four weeks connect for this manager. Synthesis, not attendance.

Team Development Plan. A 90-day plan for the manager's actual team, presented at the capstone to peers and their business unit leader.

Why it matters

Three places the return shows up.

Success is not measured by seat time. It shows up in team engagement, in case volume, and in the bench of leaders ready to step up.

Team performance

Managers who lead better

Managers are the single greatest influence on team engagement. Voyage builds goal setting, 1:1s, and coaching before damage is done. Tracked in Culture Amp engagement scores.

Lower ER and EOA cases

Fewer preventable failures

The most avoidable cases start with a manager never taught to handle a difficult conversation or to recognize when something must be escalated. The first 60 days are the most direct risk-reduction investment Vanderbilt can make.

Promote from within

A deeper leadership bench

One standard across all 39 business units means graduates can be compared on the same evidence when promotion and succession decisions are made.
