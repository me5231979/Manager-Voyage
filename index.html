One framework behind every component

Fifteen behaviors. Four categories. One standard.

Everything rests on Gary Yukl’s taxonomy of effective leadership behavior. Foundations teaches it. The cohort practices it. The portal equips it. The pulse measures it.

Task-oriented

Get the work done, through your team.

Relations-oriented

Take care of your people.

Change-oriented

Make things better.

External

Connect your team to Vanderbilt.

The fifteen behaviors
Task-oriented
  • Clarifying (say it)
  • Planning (plan it)
  • Monitoring operations (check it)
  • Problem solving (fix it)
Relations-oriented
  • Supporting (listen and help)
  • Developing (grow them)
  • Recognizing (thank them)
  • Empowering (trust them)
Change-oriented
  • Advocating change (explain the why)
  • Envisioning change (describe where you are going)
  • Encouraging innovation (let people try)
  • Facilitating collective learning (look back)
External
  • Networking (know the people)
  • External monitoring (watch for what is coming)
  • Representing (speak up for your team)

Measures of impact

Short. Behavioral. Honest.

Seat time proves nothing. We measure reach from enrollment, and change from the same six statements asked before the program and 90 days after.

1 · Pre

Before Manager Voyage.

Six statements. A 1 to 5 scale, never to consistently.

2 · Post

Ninety days after.

The same six. The difference is the measure.

3 · Sustain

Six months, optional.

Does the change stick?

The six statements
BehaviorPulse item
Create clarityI make priorities, expectations, and what good looks like clear.Clarifying, planning
Coach and developI give timely feedback and help people build capability through their work.Monitoring operations, developing
Build trustI create an environment where people can speak up, ask questions, and learn from mistakes.Supporting, recognizing, networking
Empower othersI give people appropriate ownership and authority to make decisions.Empowering, encouraging innovation
Address issuesI address performance, people, or process problems early and constructively.Problem solving, monitoring operations, representing
Lead changeI connect change to the bigger picture and help my team adapt, learn, and try new ways of working.Advocating change, collective learning, external monitoring

What success looks like.

  • 80% of M1 to M5 managers enrolled.
  • Managers improving on at least four of the six.
  • The behavior that gained most, and the one that needs more support.
  • Next: direct reports answer the same six.

Describe the pulse as informed by Yukl’s leadership-behavior framework. Never as a validated Yukl instrument.

Component 01

Compliance courses.

What the law requires, where the team works. Assigned on day one.

RequiredDays 1 to 60 · Annual
What is assigned, by location

Legal courses carry a deadline. The rest are Vanderbilt policy. Supervisors take both versions.

    Every location

      Logistics
      • Assignment. Automatic in Oracle on role entry. No enrollment step.
      • Delivery. OpenSesame in Oracle Learning, each with a knowledge check.
      • Deadlines. Legal courses in 30 days, advisory in 60. My voyage flags anything at risk.
      • Renewal. Annual, or biennial where the state allows.
      • Record. Oracle holds the official completion.
