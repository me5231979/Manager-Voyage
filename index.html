Why it exists · The unmanaged window

The first sixty days are left to chance.

Every day Vanderbilt promotes or hires a new manager, a clock starts. In the first 60 days that manager sets expectations and establishes patterns that define their leadership for years.

Without structure, the result is predictable: compliance gaps, avoidable Employee Relations and Equal Opportunity and Access cases, and managers who were never shown what the role actually requires.

Manager Voyage is not a training program. It is a management standard: the first structured, sequenced, institution-wide development journey for every Vanderbilt manager.

The architecture · Four components, one journey

Two are required. Two are always available.

Oracle Learning assigns both required components the day a manager's role begins. No manual enrollment, no dropped handoff. Completing both unlocks the cohort. The Portal is open from Day 1 and never taken away.

01Required

Compliance Courses

Days 1 to 60 · Self-paced

  • 20 state-specific courses, legal and advisory
  • Auto-assigned by work location in Oracle
  • Delivered through OpenSesame
  • The floor, not the bar
02Required

Manager Responsibilities Course

Days 1 to 60 · About 4 hours total

  • 30-minute foundation course, then 22 micro modules under 15 minutes each
  • Four tracks: Systems, People, Processes, Vanderbilt & Nashville
  • Simulators and scenario studios, not slide decks
03Always on

Manager Portal

Day 1 onward · Never expires

  • Every template, framework, policy link, and contact
  • Same hub for a new manager and a graduate from two years ago
  • Grows with each cohort cycle
04Optional

Four-Week Cohort

Quarterly · 100 seats

  • In-person kickoff and capstone
  • Blended weeks with AI rehearsal and peer labs
  • Open to any manager who has finished both required components

How the journey flows · The system does the work

What a manager experiences.

Compliance tells a manager what the law requires. The Manager Responsibilities Course tells them what Vanderbilt requires. The cohort turns a compliant manager into a capable one.

  1. Day 1Assigned on arrival.

    The HR record shows a management role. Oracle assigns both required components. Portal access opens.

  2. Days 1 to 30Systems and story.

    Foundation course, Systems track, and Vanderbilt & Nashville track. The manager can approve, hire, and explain the mission.

  3. Days 31 to 60People and process.

    People and Processes tracks, every simulator passed. The manager can run a 1:1, handle a leave request, and document a concern.

  4. Day 60Recorded and invited.

    Completion recorded against the job profile in Oracle. The next quarterly cohort invitation follows.

  5. Cohort and beyondFrom compliant to capable.

    Four weeks of live sessions, AI practice, and peer labs. The Portal stays with the manager for good.

Component 04 · The four-week cohort

Where skill development happens.

One hundred managers, once a quarter. Each week pairs a core module with AI conversation rehearsal in Yoodli, a facilitated peer lab in SparkWise, and a section of the manager's own Playbook. FLH-built AI agents are available any hour for guidance and prep.

Week 1

What it means to manage

In-person kickoff. Management identity and psychological safety. Establishes the why before the how.

Week 2

Goals, 1:1s, difficult conversations

Rehearsed 1:1s and hard conversations. Builds daily operating discipline.

Week 3

Coaching and mentoring

Delegation and coach-or-correct labs. Shifts the manager from directing to developing.

Week 4

Performance and developing talent

Performance conversations, then the in-person capstone. Closes the loop from learning to leading.

Manager Playbook

A written personal operating system: philosophy, 1:1 framework, feedback, delegation, performance practice.

Integrated Management Model

A one-page model of how the four weeks connect for this manager. Synthesis, not attendance.

Team Development Plan

A 90-day plan for the manager's actual team, presented to peers and their business unit leader.

Impact · Three places the return shows up

Success is not measured by seat time.

Team performance

Managers who lead better

Managers are the single greatest influence on team engagement. Voyage builds goal setting, 1:1s, and coaching before damage is done. Tracked in Culture Amp engagement scores.

Lower ER and EOA cases

Fewer preventable failures

The most avoidable cases start with a manager never taught to handle a difficult conversation or escalate. The first 60 days are the most direct risk-reduction investment Vanderbilt can make.

Promote from within

A deeper leadership bench

One standard across all 39 business units means graduates can be compared on the same evidence when promotion and succession decisions are made.
