    Topic 1 of 6 · The basics

    Twelve weeks, job protected.

    • WhatA federal law. Up to 12 weeks off in 12 months for health or family, unpaid, job and health coverage kept.
    • WhyNobody should have to choose between a sick parent and a paycheck. The law makes the job wait.
    • HowThe Leave team checks eligibility and runs it. You hear it, route it, and plan the work.

    Topic 2 of 6 · Recognize it

    Nobody says the word FMLA.

    • WhatA leave request is any mention of health, family health, a new child, or military family. No acronym, no form.
    • WhyThe moment you hear it, Vanderbilt’s clock starts. Missing it is the most common FMLA failure.
    • HowOne question in your head: is it one of the four? Yes, route it. No, handle it. You never judge whether it qualifies.

    Topic 3 of 6 · Where it goes

    Where should it go?

    • WhatFour doors. The right one depends on what happened, not on how sure you are.
    • WhyEach door has a team behind it that decides, documents and protects. You do not have to be the expert.
    • HowHear it, pick the door, the same day. Tell your HCM. Then plan the work.

    Topic 4 of 6 · Origami

    Origami is where the leave lives.

    • WhatVanderbilt’s leave and claims system. Request, certification, approved dates and return, all in one place.
    • WhyOne record, owned by the Leave team, means the medical detail never has to pass through you.
    • HowSend them to Origami the day you hear it. Watch for the approved dates. Record time with the codes you are given.
    Never
    • Ask for a diagnosis or the paperwork
    • Decide eligibility
    • Wait for a better time
    • Count approved leave as attendance
    If a certification reaches you
    • Forward it unread to fmlaprocessing@vanderbilt.edu
    • Delete your copy
