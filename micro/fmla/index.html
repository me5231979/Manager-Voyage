    Topic 1 of 8 · What FMLA is

    What FMLA is.

    The Family and Medical Leave Act is a federal law. It lets an eligible employee take time off for a serious health or family reason and keep their job and their health insurance.

    • 12weeks of leaveIn a 12-month period. Up to 26 weeks to care for an injured service member.
    • 12months to qualify12 months at Vanderbilt and 1,250 hours worked in the past year.
    • ✓job protectedYou come back to the same job or an equivalent one. Health insurance continues.
    • $unpaid by lawYour Vanderbilt paid time, like sick time, PTO or paid parental leave, can run at the same time.

    Myth or fact? Four statements

    0 of 4 decided.

    • Your own health
      A serious health condition: surgery, a chronic illness, a hospital stay.
    • A family member
      Caring for a spouse, child or parent with a serious health condition.
    • A new child
      Birth, adoption or foster placement, for either parent, within the first year.
    • Military family
      A family member’s active duty, or caring for an injured service member.
    Topic 2 of 8 · How FMLA leave works

    How FMLA leave works.

    Leave can be taken three ways. The Leave team approves the type and the dates. Pay, benefits and your job are handled the same way for all three.

    • Continuous
      One block of timeSix weeks after surgery. Twelve weeks with a new baby.
    • Intermittent
      Separate days or hoursChemo every other Tuesday. A migraine morning. Each one is leave.
    • Reduced schedule
      Fewer hours for a whileHalf days for the first month back.
    • While you are outHealth insurance continues. You keep paying your share of the premium.
    • Your payPaid time runs alongside the leave when you have it. Then it is unpaid.
    • Coming backSame job, or an equivalent one. Approved leave never counts against you.

    Topic 3 of 8 · How to request FMLA leave

    How to request FMLA leave.

    For staff. Five steps, and the Leave team is with you for all of them. Ask 30 days ahead when you can. When you cannot, ask as soon as you know.

    1. 1
      Tell your manager.In your own words. No diagnosis needed.
    2. 2
      Open the request in Origami.Or call 615-343-4125 and the Leave team opens it with you.
    3. 3
      Your provider completes the certification.Due back within 15 calendar days. It goes to the Leave team, not your manager.
    4. 4
      The Leave team decides.Eligibility within 5 business days, then the approved type and dates.
    5. 5
      Take the leave, then come back.Keep in touch about your return date. Your job is waiting.

    Topic 4 of 8 · How a manager handles a leave request

    How a manager handles a leave request.

    For managers and HCMs. Nobody says “FMLA.” They mention health, a family member’s health, a new child, or military family. That is the request.

    1. 1
      Hear it.Thank them. Do not ask what is wrong.
    2. 2
      Send it to Origami the same day.Tell your HCM.
    3. 3
      Plan coverage from the approved dates.Record time with the codes the Leave team gives you.
    • Yes, one of the four
      Send it to Origami today.“My mom had a stroke.” “The doctor wants me out three weeks.”
    • No, plain time off
      Approve it yourself.“Friday off for a wedding.” “Can I start at 7?”

    Your HCM confirms the request is in Origami, keeps the time records right, and is your first call with a question.

    Topic 6 of 8 · Hurt at work

    Hurt at work: where the employee goes.

    Medical treatment and claim administration. Both follow the employee’s primary work location, not where the injury happened.

    Workers’ Compensation supports employees through timely medical treatment, claim administration and return-to-work resources.

    1. 1
      The employee reports the injuryThey tell their supervisor the same day and need medical treatment.
    2. 2
      Where is their primary work location?Send them to the right provider for that location, below.
    3. 3
      The claim is administeredBased on the primary work location, not where the injury occurred.
    • Nashville campus
      Go to the Occupational Health Clinic (OHC).

      1211 21st Ave. South, Medical Arts Building, Suite 640, Nashville, TN 37212

      Hours: Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

      • OHC closed and you need care now: go to the VUMC Emergency Department.
      • Treated in the emergency department: follow up with OHC the next business day.
      • Follow-up, in person or telehealth: 615-936-0955.
    • Tennessee employeesoutside the Nashville campus
      Go to the nearest Vanderbilt Walk-in Clinic.
      • No Vanderbilt walk-in clinic nearby: the nearest medical facility, walk-in clinic, urgent care or emergency room, depending on the injury.
      • Tell the staff the injury is work related.
    • New York and othernon-Tennessee employees
      Go to the nearest appropriate medical facility.
      • A walk-in clinic, urgent care center or emergency room, depending on the injury.
      • Tell the staff the injury is work related.
      • Follow the workers’ compensation reporting process.
    • Traveling onVanderbilt business
      Get initial treatment at the nearest appropriate facility.
      • A walk-in clinic, urgent care center or emergency room, depending on the injury.
      • Tell the staff the injury is work related.
      • The claim is administered based on your primary work location.

    Topic 7 of 8 · Origami, the leave system

    Origami, the leave system.

    Every leave request and every work-injury claim lives in Origami, with the Leave and Workers’ Compensation team on the other side. The request, the certification, the approved dates and the return date are all there.

    1. 1
      The request goes in.The staff member opens it, or the manager opens it with them, the day it comes up.
    2. 2
      The Leave team approves.Type and dates. Everyone sees the same answer.
    3. 3
      The manager plans coverage.From those dates. The HCM keeps the time records right.

    Medical paperwork in your inbox? Forward it unread to fmlaprocessing@vanderbilt.edu and delete it. Cannot get into Origami? Call 615-343-4125.
