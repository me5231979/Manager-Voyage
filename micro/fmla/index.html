    Topic 1 of 5 · What FMLA is

    What FMLA is.

    A federal law that lets an eligible employee take time off for health or family and keep their job.

    The facts

    • 12weeks of leaveIn a 12-month period. Unpaid, job protected, health coverage kept.
    • 4covered reasonsYour own health. A spouse, child or parent. A new child. Military family.
    • 1team decidesLeave and Workers’ Compensation checks eligibility and approves it. Not you.
    • Your own health
      A serious health condition. Surgery, a chronic illness, a hospital stay.
    • A family member
      Caring for a spouse, child or parent with a serious health condition.
    • A new child
      Birth, adoption or foster placement, for either parent.
    • Military family
      A family member’s deployment, or caring for an injured service member.

    Topic 2 of 5 · How to spot a leave request

    How to spot a leave request.

    Nobody says “FMLA.” They mention health, a family member’s health, a new child, or military family. That is the request.

    1. 1
      They say it.In their own words.
    2. 2
      You ask one question.Is it one of the four?
    3. 3
      You act today.Route it, or handle it.
    • Yes, one of the four
      Send it to Origami today.“My mom had a stroke.” “The doctor wants me out three weeks.”
    • No, plain time off
      Approve it yourself.“Friday off for a wedding.” “Can I start at 7?”
