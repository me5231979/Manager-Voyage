    The thinking this week

    Frameworks you will use, not just hear.

    The operating discipline of the role, and the thinkers behind each piece of it.

    • Listen forThe difference between a fact and a story about a fact. Line one of the frame is only ever the fact.
    • Connect itThe module’s vocabulary is Expectation 01: clarifying is statement 2 on your assessment, monitoring is statement 3. A difficult conversation is clarifying, late.
    • Bring to the labThe one conversation you have been putting off. Name the person privately, the fact, and the date it happened.
    01

    Bring the real one

    The conversation you have been avoiding. In the lab you frame it in three lines with two other managers, and they tell you which line is still a hint.

    02

    Leave with it framed

    What I noticed, with dates. What I expect from here. What I need, by when. Then the question: what is in the way?

    03

    Then hold it

    This week, with your team, in private. Write the follow-up the same day. That is the assignment; the lab is the rehearsal.

    Keep it theirsThe 1:1 is Dev’s meeting.Open with their agenda. Do not ambush. But do not leave without the hard part.

    Then agreeOne next step, with a date.Something Dev does, something you do. Written in the follow-up.
