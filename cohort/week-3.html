    The thinking this week

    Frameworks you will use, not just hear.

    Coaching has a hundred years of research behind it. Five pieces of it you will use this week.

    • Listen forThe ratio. A coaching conversation is mostly questions. If you are talking more than a third of the time, you are mentoring, which is fine, but name it.
    • Connect itDeveloping is one of the fifteen behaviors under Expectation 02 your assessment did not measure. Empowering is statement 6. This week practices both.
    • Bring to the labOne decision you currently make that someone on your team could make. And the one boundary you would put around it.

    The ratio

    • Rung 1Do exactly this. New to the task, high stakes.
    • Rung 2Look into it and tell me what you find. I decide.
    • Rung 3Recommend. I decide, and I will tell you why if I disagree.
    • Rung 4Decide, and tell me before you act. One boundary, one check-in date.
    • Rung 5Decide and act. Tell me if it goes wrong. Your name is on it; mine is behind it.

    In the lab you place each person on the ladder for one real piece of work, and name the next rung up. Most people on most teams are a rung lower than they could be.

    Ask firstWhere do you want to be?What have you tried? What would you do first? At least two real questions before any advice.
