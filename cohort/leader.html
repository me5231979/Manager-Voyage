For the manager’s leader · Fifteen minutes · One question a week

Your manager is in the cohort.

Four weeks of practicing the Vanderbilt Manager Standard with their own team. This page is what we ask of you: fifteen minutes before week one, one question a week, and two hours at the capstone.

The standard your manager is measured on

Four expectations, from Gary Yukl’s research, in Vanderbilt’s words.

Yukl’s four categories of effective leadership behavior, task, relations, change and external, became four expectations of every Vanderbilt manager. Your manager rated themselves on ten statements under them before the cohort and will again at ninety days. One week of the cohort leans on each.

    Fifteen minutes, then one question a week.

    The capstone, two hours

    Your manager presents their Playbook in five minutes: the sentence at the center, the team goal, one person’s plan by name, the habit they kept. Peers challenge one goal, one move, one date. You sit with a group that is not your own and close it with one sentence: the Playbook you would most like to see in ninety days, and why.

    90

    After ninety days

    The reassessment: the same ten statements, and the team’s anonymous view of them. Ask your manager what moved. Then ask what they want to move next.
